CBS AI is a student-driven organization dedicated to bridging the gap between AI and business.

We equip CBS students with the knowledge and insights needed to navigate the rapidly evolving world of Artificial Intelligence through educational events and networking opportunities. By collaborating with industry leaders, we bring the latest developments, real-world applications, and career opportunities directly to you.

How We Build an Event

Four units, one relay. Every event passes through the same hands.

1

Partnerships

Secures the industry speaker and the sponsorships each event needs, briefed by Operations on what is required.

2

Funding

Steps in with the budget and financial backing that turn the plan into something we can run.

3

Marketing & Community

Fills the room with campaigns, social media and outreach to the CBS community.

4

Operations

Executes on the floor: logistics, setup and the experience on the day.

Inside Partnerships

What we build with companies, and the new system that keeps it running.

The work

  • Industry speakers. Practitioners from firms like Microsoft, BCG and Accenture on our stages.
  • Sponsorships. The funding and prizes that keep our events free for students.
  • Relationships that last. Partners who come back year after year, from SAP to Lovable.

Our new CRM New

  • One shared pipeline. Every company, contact and deal lives in one place.
  • Nothing gets lost. Full history on every partner, so handovers keep the context.
  • Clear ownership. Every dialogue has an owner and a next step.
